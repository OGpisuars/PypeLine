# Art style

PypeLine looks like a cozy 16-bit handheld RPG overworld that happens to be full of steam machinery. The full art direction is in roadmap Part 1 ("RENDERING" and "ART DIRECTION"). This page is the practical summary for anyone drawing for the game.

> **Original work only.** The GBA look is a style reference. Never trace, copy or closely imitate sprites, logos, fonts or sounds from Nintendo, Game Freak or any other game.

## The canvas

- The world renders at **480x320** (exactly 2x the GBA's 240x160) and is scaled to the window by a **whole number**, so every pixel stays square and sharp. Never draw at fractional positions.
- **16x16 tiles.** A plot is 16 tiles wide and 10 tall. Machines are 1x1 today; 2x2 and 3x3 machines are planned.
- View: top-down 3/4, like a handheld overworld.

## Color

- **GBA colors:** every channel is a multiple of 8 (5 bits per channel, like the real hardware). Tests check this for the UI themes.
- **Limited palettes:** at most 15 colors plus transparent per sprite or tileset.
- **Mood:** bright, saturated and cozy. Sky blues, grass greens, warm brass and copper for steam machinery, and one accent color per wire type.
- The current colors are provisional and live in code: `src/engine/palette.rs` (world), `src/engine/sprites.rs` (sprite color keys) and `src/engine/themes.rs` (UI themes).

## Shading and line

- Flat color ramps: 3–4 shades per material.
- A 1-pixel dark outline (`#282030`) around sprites.
- One light source, from the top left.
- **No** dithering, gradients or anti-aliasing inside the game world. (The UI and the company logo are drawn at full window resolution and may be smooth.)

## Animation

- Short loops of 2–4 frames: belts, steam puffs, gears, wire pulses.
- Animations follow the simulation tick where they show real work (belts move as fast as items do), so what you see is what the factory is doing.

## UI

- Dialogue-box style windows: rounded borders, a cream fill and dark text in the default "Classic" theme. Five more themes are in Settings.
- Code uses a monospace font (JetBrains Mono by default). Fonts must allow bundling in a commercial game (for example the SIL Open Font License) and be listed in `ASSET_LICENSES.md`.

## Where art lives today

All sprites are currently **drawn in code** as small text grids (`src/engine/sprites.rs`), so the game ships no image files yet. During the art pass, real sprite sheets will go in `assets/sprites/` and replace them one by one. Sound effects are generated by a small synthesizer (`src/audio/sfx.rs`); music is in `assets/audio/music/`.

## Contributing art

Open an issue first with a mock-up. Include the source file (Aseprite or a layered PNG), keep within the palette rules above, and confirm the work is your own so it can be listed in `ASSET_LICENSES.md`.
